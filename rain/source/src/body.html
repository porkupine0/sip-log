  • Lock your phone. The rain keeps playing with the screen off, and on silent mode too. Use the side buttons for volume.
  • No loop to hear. Your phone makes a new stretch of rain about every 75 seconds and blends it in over 8 seconds, so there is no seam and no pattern that repeats.
  • Lock-screen controls pause and resume it without unlocking.

Keep it offline

Web link:

iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it once while online. After that it works in airplane mode, because the rain is made on the phone and nothing is downloaded.

Android: open the link in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Install app.

If the rain stops when you lock the phone, play it from a Safari tab instead of the Home Screen icon.

Made on your phone, no recordings. Nothing leaves the device.
